IT Helpdesk Triage Agent

An agentic ticket-triage system for a corporate internal helpdesk: classifies incoming IT tickets, retrieves relevant knowledge, decides whether the issue can receive a documented first response or needs a human specialist, and drafts that response.

5 ticketsSecurity, Other, and no-KB-match cases

Product guardrail

Safety, security, and privileged-access tickets must never be incorrectly resolved with a canned response.

The product is intentionally conservative: an unnecessary escalation is preferable to confidently giving the wrong automated resolution in a high-risk case.

Design decisions

  • Explicit multi-stage flowClassification, retrieval, decision, and drafting are separate stages, making the system easier to inspect and debug than a single large prompt.
  • Simple retrieval for a small KBWith eight articles, category-scoped keyword matching is transparent and avoids unnecessary retrieval infrastructure in the prototype.
  • Root-cause check before resolutionA topical KB match is not enough; the decision stage checks whether the article actually addresses the issue described.
  • Conservative escalationSafety, security, privileged access, possible outages, failed troubleshooting, no-match cases, and uncertainty route to a human.
  • Free-tier prototypeThe project stays inspectable and avoids building a production ticketing integration that is outside the v1 scope.
